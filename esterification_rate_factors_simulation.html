Temperature → faster, more energetic collisions. Catalyst → lowers the energy a collision needs to succeed, and (exaggerated here for visibility) makes the approach angle a little more forgiving too — at 100% almost every collision reacts. Changing a starting concentration restarts the batch. (Pressure isn't included here since this is a liquid-phase reaction — see the separate gas-pressure simulation for that.)
