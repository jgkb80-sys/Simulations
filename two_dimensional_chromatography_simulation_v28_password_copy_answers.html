6. Use chromatogram 1 and the ruler to determine two Rf values: the coloured spot with the highest affinity for the mobile phase and the coloured spot with the least affinity for the mobile phase.

The ruler starts at the bottom edge of the paper and is graduated every 0.1 cm, with longer marks every 0.5 cm and 1.0 cm. Read it carefully and use differences in readings to determine distances travelled.

Least mobile-phase affinity spot
The ruler zero is deliberately not aligned with the origin line. Measure the ruler reading at the origin, solvent front and spot centres, then subtract the origin reading before calculating Rf.
